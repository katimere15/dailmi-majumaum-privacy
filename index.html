개인 배려카드는 기기 우선 저장공유 데이터는 Firebase 저장광고·분석 SDK 없음

개인 배려카드의 제목·메모·상기 설정은 마주마음 서버로 전송하지 않습니다. 연인 연결 뒤 보낸 마음카드, 후보 날짜, 공유 일정과 준비 체크리스트는 두 사람이 함께 사용하도록 서버에 저장합니다. 공유 데이터는 전송 및 저장 과정에서 암호화되지만 현재 종단간 암호화 방식은 아닙니다.

계정을 삭제하면 프로필과 등록 기기 데이터는 운영 데이터베이스에서 즉시 삭제되고, 연결된 두 사람의 공유 데이터는 접근이 즉시 중단된 뒤 7일 후 삭제됩니다. 데이터 내보내기 ZIP은 암호화되지 않으므로 저장·공유 위치를 주의해서 선택해야 합니다.

1. 개인정보처리자와 적용 범위

Dailmi(이하 “운영자”)는 개인정보 보호법 제30조에 따라 마주마음 앱에서 개인정보가 어떻게 처리되는지 안내하기 위해 이 방침을 공개합니다. 이 방침은 마주마음 Android 앱과 앱 운영을 위한 Firebase 백엔드에 적용됩니다.

마주마음은 대한민국을 첫 서비스 국가로 하며, 일반 채팅·실시간 위치 공유·연락처 수집 기능을 제공하지 않습니다. 앱에 Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics 또는 광고 SDK를 포함하지 않습니다.

3. 기기 내 저장, 알림과 Android 백업

개인 배려카드와 그 알림 설정은 앱 전용 로컬 저장소에 보관합니다. 공유 마음카드와 일정은 빠른 화면 표시를 위해 기기에도 임시 저장될 수 있으며, 연결 해제·차단·계정 삭제 때 앱 안의 공유 사본을 지웁니다.

개인 배려카드 알림은 기기에서 예약합니다. 잠금화면 알림에는 카드 제목·메모를 넣지 않고 “저장해 둔 개인 배려 카드를 확인해보세요”와 같은 일반 문구만 사용합니다. Android 알림 권한과 시스템 설정에 따라 알림이 표시되지 않을 수 있습니다.

현재 앱은 Android 운영체제 자동 백업을 명시적으로 차단하지 않습니다. 따라서 기기·계정 백업 설정에 따라 앱 전용 로컬 데이터가 Android 백업 제공자에게 저장되거나 새 기기로 복원될 수 있습니다. 앱을 삭제하더라도 이미 만들어진 운영체제 백업 또는 사용자가 내보낸 파일까지 앱이 직접 지울 수는 없습니다.

4. 연결한 연인에게 공개되는 정보

마주마음은 사용자가 직접 선택한 한 명의 연인과 공유 기능을 제공하기 위해 다음 정보를 그 상대에게 공개합니다.

받는 사람공개 항목목적보유·이용 기간
사용자가 초대하거나 초대를 수락해 연결한 연인 1명별명과 연결 상태, 보낸 마음카드 문구·후보 날짜·공개 및 응답 상태, 확정된 공유 일정, 장소·메모와 준비 체크리스트두 사람의 마음 표현, 날짜 합의, 공동 준비와 일정 실행앱에서 연결되어 있는 동안. 연결 종료 시 즉시 접근 중단하며 서버 공유 데이터는 7일 후 삭제

마음카드 문구는 상대가 긁기 동작을 하기 전부터 전달을 위해 서버와 상대 앱에 처리될 수 있고, 긁기 동작은 화면에서 내용을 공개하는 사용자 경험입니다. 개인 배려카드 원문과 개인 알림은 연인에게 보내지 않습니다.

법령에 특별한 규정이 있거나 수사기관·법원이 적법한 절차에 따라 요구하는 경우에는 필요한 범위에서 정보를 제공할 수 있습니다. 운영자는 개인정보를 판매하지 않습니다.

계정 삭제 전에 사용자가 외부로 내보낸 파일, 상대가 별도로 저장한 내용, 운영체제 백업, Google의 법정·백업 보관 범위는 앱에서 직접 삭제할 수 없습니다.

English summary

Private care-card content and reminder settings are stored primarily on your device and are not sent to Majumaum servers. Heart cards, proposed dates, shared events, and preparation checklists are stored in Firebase so that the connected couple can use them together. Shared data is encrypted in transit and at rest by the provider, but it is not currently end-to-end encrypted. Exported ZIP files are not encrypted.

1. Controller and scope

Dailmi (“we”) publishes this Privacy Policy to explain how the Majumaum Android app and its Firebase backend process personal information. Majumaum launches first in South Korea and does not provide general chat, real-time location sharing, or contact collection. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or an advertising SDK.

2. Information we process and why

  • Account and authentication: Firebase UID, Google account email, and, when Google supplies them, profile name and photo, provider information, authentication metadata, IP address, and user agent. These support sign-in, account management, reauthentication, and abuse prevention.
  • Profile: nickname, account timestamps, and current couple-connection ID.
  • Private care cards: category, title, note, timestamps, reminder time, and one-time, weekly, monthly, or yearly recurrence. These stay in app-private local storage and are not uploaded to Majumaum servers.
  • Connection and invitations: both users’ UIDs and nicknames, connection state and timestamps, a secret-peppered hash of the invitation code, creator, expiry, use time, and attempt count. The raw invitation code is not stored.
  • Heart cards and date agreement: sender and recipient identifiers and names, message, up to three proposed dates, reveal and response status, alternative date, and relevant timestamps.
  • Shared calendar and preparation: event title, date and time, text location entered by a user, note, status, creator, and checklist content, assignee, completion status, and timestamps. Majumaum does not collect device geolocation.
  • Safety settings: reception pause, blocked user ID and nickname, disconnection status, and scheduled deletion time.
  • Push and integrity: FCM token, Firebase installation ID, Android platform, App Check tokens, and Play Integrity attestation material.
  • Technical service data: IP address, user agent, app ID and version, request time, server-function requests, errors, and security logs generated through Firebase and Google Cloud.
  • Support: your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send.

Account and profile information is required for account-based features. Refusing it prevents sign-in and couple sharing. Other content is provided when you choose to use its feature.

3. On-device storage, reminders, and Android backup

Private care cards and their reminders are stored in app-private local storage. Shared cards and events may also be cached locally for display and are cleared from the app when you disconnect, block, or delete the account. Care-card reminders are scheduled on-device and use generic lock-screen text without the card title or note.

The current Android app does not explicitly disable operating-system automatic backup. Depending on device and account settings, local app data may therefore be backed up by the Android backup provider or restored to another device. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove an existing operating-system backup or files you exported.

4. Information shared with your connected partner

Your chosen connected partner can receive your nickname and connection status, heart-card content, proposed dates and response state, confirmed shared events, locations and notes you enter, and preparation checklists. This sharing provides the core expression, agreement, and preparation features. Access ends immediately when the connection ends; shared server data is deleted after a seven-day deletion queue.

A heart-card message may be processed by the server and the recipient’s app before the recipient performs the scratch gesture; the gesture controls its reveal in the interface. Private care-card content and reminders are not sent to the partner. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose the minimum information required by a valid legal demand.

5. Service providers

Google LLC and its Google Cloud contractual subprocessors provide Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions for Firebase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase App Check, Play Integrity, infrastructure, security, and technical support. The current list is available at Google Cloud Subprocessors.

6. International processing

Firebase Authentication runs only in U.S. data centers. Google LLC processes Google account identifiers and basic profile data, Firebase UID, authentication and security information, IP addresses, and user agents in the United States when you sign in, reauthenticate, or manage the account over encrypted connections. Google states that Authentication IP logs are kept for a few weeks and other authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after customer-initiated user deletion.

FCM, App Check, and Google Cloud operational or support data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google or its subprocessors operate facilities. This may include Firebase installation IDs, FCM tokens, attestation material and tokens, IP addresses, and app, device, request, and security information. Google states that Firebase installation IDs are removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after the relevant deletion API call.

Primary shared content in Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions execution are configured for the Seoul, South Korea region asia-northeast3. Firebase may nevertheless process technical information through global infrastructure and support operations.

You may refuse international processing by not installing or signing in, or by deleting the account after sign-up. Because this processing is necessary for current authentication and shared features, refusing it means those features cannot be used. See Firebase privacy inquiries.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Profile, safety settings, and registered device tokens are kept until account deletion. Block records remain until you unblock the user or delete the account.
  • Active couple data, heart cards, events, and checklists become inaccessible immediately after disconnection, blocking, or account deletion and are deleted from the active database after seven days.
  • An invitation becomes unusable after 24 hours or one use. At present, its record remains until the creating user deletes the account. Only a secret-peppered hash, not the raw code, is stored.
  • Local data remains until you delete an item, disconnect where applicable, delete the account or app data, or uninstall. Backup copies follow the backup provider’s policy.
  • An unencrypted export ZIP is created in app cache and may remain until the operating system or app cache removes it. Copies saved or sent elsewhere remain under your control and the selected provider’s policy.
  • Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after resolution unless a longer period is needed for security, disputes, or law.
  • Firebase provider data follows the service-specific periods in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

8. Export and account deletion

“Data & Safety → Export data” creates a ZIP containing JSON and CSV versions of your private care cards, profile, and currently accessible shared cards, events, and checklists. The file is created in temporary device storage and passed to the Android share sheet; Dailmi does not separately receive it. The ZIP is not encrypted.

“Data & Safety → Permanently delete account” requires Google reauthentication. It starts immediate removal of the profile, registered-device records, and Firebase Authentication account from active systems, immediately ends the connection, schedules shared data for deletion after seven days, and clears Majumaum local data and private reminders. Files you exported, copies independently saved by the partner, operating-system backups, and provider retention required by law or backup cycles are not directly erased by the app.

Heart cards and shared events are not currently end-to-end encrypted. Server-side processing and access by an authorized operational environment remain technically possible. Private care cards use app-private local storage but are not protected by a separate user-password-based file encryption layer. Use a secure device lock.
